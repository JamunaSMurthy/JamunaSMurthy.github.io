Proposes a multimodal sarcasm detection framework integrating text (Enhanced-BERT), image, and audio features. A novel adaptive fusion strategy combined with SarcasNet-99 achieves 97.3% accuracy on large-scale video datasets, outperforming state-of-the-art models in sarcasm detection.
